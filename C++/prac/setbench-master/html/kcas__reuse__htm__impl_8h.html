◆ tagptr_t

typedef intptr_t tagptr_t

Descriptor reuse macros seqbits_t corresponds to the seqBits field of the descriptor. it contains the mutable fields of the descriptor and a sequence number. the width, offset and mask for the sequence number is defined below. this sequence number width, offset and mask are also shared by tagptr_t.

in particular, for any tagptr_t x and seqbits_t y, the sequence numbers in x and y are equal iff x&MASK_SEQ == y&MASK_SEQ (despite differing types).

tagptr_t consists of a triple <seq, tid, testbit>. these three fields are defined by the TAGPTR_ macros below.
